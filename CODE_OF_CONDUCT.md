# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about the physics, not the person making the argument.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about a coordinate convention,
  not about anyone's competence.
- Give criticism that can be acted on. "The shadow is wrong" is not useful;
  "`b_crit` comes out 2% low at `a/M = 0.9`" is.
- Accept correction gracefully. This project has already shipped two bugs that
  passed every numerical test and were only caught by looking at the picture.
  Nobody's judgement here is beyond checking.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, unwanted attention, publishing
someone's private information, and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, commit messages and any other
project space, and to anyone taking part — including maintainers.

## Reporting

Email **iamzishaan@gmail.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept
confidential. Expect an acknowledgement within a few days.

Consequences scale with the behaviour, from a private word through to a
permanent ban from the project's spaces. The maintainer decides, and will say
why.
